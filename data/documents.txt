Hyderabad is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of Telangana. Known historically as the City of Pearls, Hyderabad has evolved into a leading global information technology and biotechnology hub alongside Cyberabad and HITEC City. TITLE: Telangana - Heritage, Culture, and Geography
LANGUAGE: English
CATEGORY: Cities & Culture
CONTENT:
Telangana is a dynamic state in southern India, formed in 2014. The state is bounded by Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh. Major rivers including the Godavari and Krishna flow through its plateau terrain, nourishing agriculture and historic settlements. Telangana is famous for the architectural legacy of the Kakatiya dynasty, Warangal fort, the Thousand Pillar Temple, and the joyous floral festival of Bathukamma. Hyderabad serves as its administrative, cultural, and economic epicenter.

LANGUAGE: English
CATEGORY: Science & Space
CONTENT:
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is the national space agency of India, headquartered in Bengaluru. ISRO has achieved extraordinary milestones in space science and satellite technology, including the historic Chandrayaan moon missions and the Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan). Operating from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota, ISRO provides vital satellite communications, earth observation, remote sensing, and navigation services, propelling India to the forefront of global aerospace exploration.
